SMS Expense Tracker

The Problem

Manual expense tracking is tedious and error-prone. Most people don't log transactions consistently, leading to incomplete financial records and hours wasted on monthly reconciliation.

The Solution

  • Webhook receives SMS from bank notifications in real-time
  • Gemini AI parses transaction details (amount, merchant, type)
  • Groq serves as fallback for reliability
  • Auto-categorizes as CREDIT or DEBIT
  • Logs to Google Sheets with zero manual input
Workflow Flow
📩 SMS → 🔗 Webhook → 🧠 AI Parse → 🔀 Route → 📊 Sheets

LinkedIn Content Automation

The Problem

Creating consistent LinkedIn content is time-consuming. Watching videos, extracting insights, formatting posts, and publishing takes 45+ minutes per post — leading to inconsistent posting and missed opportunities.

The Solution

  • Tracks YouTube videos you watch via webhook
  • Extracts transcripts automatically
  • AI filters for relevance to your niche (AI/ML/Tech)
  • Smart chunking for long videos
  • Generates engaging posts with casual, storytelling tone
  • WhatsApp approval before auto-posting to LinkedIn
